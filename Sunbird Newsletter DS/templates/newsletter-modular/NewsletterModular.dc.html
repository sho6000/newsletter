Product update Sunbird RC v2.1.0

Sunbird RC is wallet ready

Sunbird RC is how you stand up a registry and start issuing digital credentials without building the hard parts yourself. You describe what you are recording; RC handles the schemas, the signing, the storage and the verification.

The short version of v2.1.0: credentials from your deployment now work with wallets you did not have to build.

Built on the standards the ecosystem settled on

OpenID for Verifiable Credentials for issuance and presentation, and W3C VC Data Model 2.0 alongside 1.1. All opt in: no database migration, no change to what you already run.

Any wallet, not just one you built

Any conformant wallet can now receive an RC credential and present it back to an RC verifier, with nothing Sunbird-specific at the wallet end. Tested against wallets built outside the Sunbird ecosystem.

Holders share only what is needed

Selective-disclosure formats, including the mobile document format behind digital driving licences. Prove you are over 18 without handing over the rest of the credential.
