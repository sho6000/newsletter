Sunbird Spark

Sunbird Ed (the DPG for learning, skilling and capacity building) has evolved to its newest version — Sunbird Spark. Spark offers best-in-class DPG capabilities for the learning and skilling domains, in an efficient shape and form for all classes of adopters, while retaining the extensibility, scalability and reliability of Sunbird Ed — the core DPG that powered DIKSHA in India.

What's new across the blocks

Spark — what shipped

A quick look at what's new in Sunbird Spark, and what's coming next:

Sunbird Spark just shipped v1.0.2 (8 July 2026), and the roadmap for the next two months adds further depth on personalization and AI.

  1. Personalized Learning Journeys & Competency Frameworks

    Spark will be able to assess a learner against a defined set of skills, pinpoint exactly which competencies they're missing, and build a learning journey to close those gaps. Once a competency framework is mapped to Sunbird's taxonomy, Spark will automatically recommend the right content — no manual curation needed.

  2. Richer, More Flexible Assessments

    QuML assessments now support all question types — Fill in the Blanks, Match the Following, Sequence Ordering and Reordering — both online and offline — plus a new True/False type. Authors can save questions in four languages by default, and the editors have been rebuilt in React with full right-to-left layout.

  3. AI at the Core

    Spark has enabled MCP integration, providing a standard way to connect with AI tools and agents. The AI pipeline is being built as core infrastructure — the backbone for running AI jobs at scale — starting with captions, transcripts and configurable metadata tagging.

  4. Under the Hood

    v1.0.2 also strengthened the platform's foundations: a new on-demand tool lets operators refresh search indexes without redeploying the system, and 43 security vulnerabilities tied to component upgrades were resolved across the platform.
