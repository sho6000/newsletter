One-line: the flat container behind story blocks, stat panels and callouts.

```jsx
<Card tone="marigold"><h3>What is Sunbird?</h3><p>…</p></Card>
```

8px radius, 1px hairline border, near-invisible shadow. Colour-block cards (`clay`, `vermilion`, `sky`) come from the deck's four-up comparison layouts — use at most two tones side by side.
