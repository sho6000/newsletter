# SPOTLIGHT

Sunbird RC is Wallet-Ready

Sunbird RC is a powerful building block that allows you to quickly configure and deploy electronic Registries, as well as Verifiable Credentials based workflows (issuance and validation). V2.1.0 of this open-source, MIT licensed DPG now also supports wallet integration, making it an even more powerful offering. Sunbird RC is part of several DPIs such as CoWIN and DIKSHA in India and is now seeing global adoption.
Sunbird RC is part of DIVOC, which has issued over 2 billion COVID-19 vaccination certificates across India and four other countries. RC also powers NSDC’s skilling registry of 200 million entries and has credentialed 20 million officials on iGOT Karmayogi.

# WHAT’S NEW

Sunbird RC just shipped v2.1.0. 
Credentials from your deployment now work with wallets you did not have to build.

## Built on the standards the ecosystem settled on:
RC now speaks OpenID for Verifiable Credentials for issuance and presentation, and supports W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model 2.0 alongside 1.1. Your credentials point the same direction the rest of the world is moving. All of it is opt in: no database migration, no change to what you already run.

## Any wallet, not just one you built:
Until now, getting an RC credential into a wallet meant building against Sunbird's own APIs. Someone had to own that integration, and it only worked for the one wallet it was written for. Now any conformant wallet can receive a credential and present it back to an RC verifier, with nothing Sunbird specific at the wallet end. This has been tested against wallets built outside the Sunbird ecosystem.

## Holders share only what is needed:
Credentials can be issued in formats that support selective disclosure, including the mobile document format behind digital driving licences. Someone can prove they are over 18, or that they hold a qualification, without handing over the rest of the credential. Less exposure for the holder, less data for you to protect.

What can you build with it?
Anywhere a credential is issued once and has to be trusted every time it is shown afterwards, there is a registry functioning underneath. Vaccination and health records, training and skilling certificates, learner credentials that follow a student between institutions, professional licences, farmer and land records organ pledge registries, proof of participation in a programme - The shape is always the same: a verified record becomes something the holder carries, instead of a document they have to request again each time someone asks for it.
A number of these already exist as reference solutions and working demos, so you can follow a credential from issuance through to verification before committing to build anything of your own. Check out the SB RC demos to find out more.
